• Crop Rotation
    Sustainable Farming Practice: Involves planting different crops in a planned sequence across growing seasons.

    Improves Soil Fertility: Helps balance soil nutrients, reducing the need for chemical fertilizers.

    Reduces Erosion: Strengthens soil structure, minimizing soil loss from wind and water.

    Controls Pests and Diseases: Disrupts pest and disease cycles by changing crop types regularly.

    Enhances Crop Yields: Healthier soil and fewer pests lead to higher crop productivity.

    Suppresses Weeds: Rotating crops with different growth habits helps control weed populations.

    Long-term Productivity: Promotes sustainable farming by maintaining soil health and environmental quality.

    Requires Careful Planning: Crop cycles must be planned based on soil conditions, climate, and crop requirements.

Water prediction in agriculture forecasts crop water needs using factors like soil moisture, temperature, humidity, wind speed, and solar radiation.
