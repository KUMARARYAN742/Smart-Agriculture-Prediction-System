Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation (KALIA)

A flagship initiative by the Odisha government aimed at providing direct financial assistance to enhance livelihoods and reduce distress in the agricultural sector.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Must be a resident of Odisha
  • Small and marginal farmers
  • Landless agricultural laborers

    • Benefits:

  • Interest-free crop loans up to ₹50,000.
  • Life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh and personal accident cover of ₹2 lakh.

    • Application Process:

  • Apply online through the official KALIA portal linked below
  • Submit the application form with required documents (Aadhar Card, Address Proof, Bank Account details, Residential Address, Photograph, Phone Number)
  • Track the application status through the portal

    • Apply Now
    Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY)

    This scheme promotes entrepreneurship in agriculture by providing financial assistance to youth and women for setting up agri-business enterprises in Odisha.

    Eligibility Criteria:

  • Youth and women entrepreneurs in Odisha.
  • Applicants aged between 18 to 45 years.
  • Must have a viable project proposal in agriculture or allied sectors.

    • Benefits:

  • 40% subsidy on the project cost, up to a maximum of ₹50 lakh.
  • Special provisions for rural youth and women.

    • Application Process:

  • Apply online through the Directorate of Agriculture and Food Production, Odisha website linked below
  • Submit a detailed project proposal online.
  • Track application status and avail subsidies through the portal.

    • Apply Now
    Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

    PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance to farmers, protecting them from financial losses due to crop failure caused by natural calamities, pests, or diseases.

    Eligibility Criteria:

  • All farmers, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers growing notified crops.
  • Must have an insurable interest in the crop.
  • Must have a valid Kisan Credit Card (KCC) or obtain one within six months of enrollment.

    • Benefits:

  • Insurance coverage for crop losses from pre-sowing to post-harvest.
  • Low premium rates: 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, and 5% for commercial crops.

    • Application Process:

  • Apply online through the PMFBY portal linked below
  • Submit crop details and required documents online.

    • Apply Now
    Biju Krushak Kalyan Yojana (BKKY)

    A health insurance scheme designed to provide financial support to farmers and their families during medical emergencies.

    Eligibility Criteria:

  • Resident of Odisha.
  • Farmer or agricultural worker with a valid farmer ID.

    • Benefits:

  • Health insurance coverage up to ₹1 lakh per family per annum.
  • Coverage extended up to ₹3 lakh for critical illnesses.

    • Application Process:

  • Enroll through the nearest Jan Seva Kendra or authorized government centers.
  • Provide necessary documents like farmer ID, proof of residence, and Aadhaar card.

    • Apply Now
    Soil Health Card Scheme

    A central initiative implemented in Odisha to provide farmers with soil health cards, helping them make informed decisions about soil nutrient management and improving crop productivity.

    Eligibility Criteria:

  • All farmers in Odisha.
  • Farmers with access to agricultural land.

    • Benefits:

  • Free soil testing and issuance of a Soil Health Card.
  • Detailed information on soil nutrient status and recommendations for fertilizers.

    • Application Process:

  • Apply online through the Soil Health Card portal
  • Register and submit details of the land for soil testing.

    • Apply Now
